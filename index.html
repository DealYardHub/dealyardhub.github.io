About DealYard Hub

Building Trust Through Every Service

DealYard Hub is a modern service company dedicated to connecting people through secure, reliable, and customer-focused solutions. From financial transactions and parcel delivery to flight bookings and mobile phone solutions, our goal is to simplify everyday needs with professionalism and integrity.

Founded by Nigel Takunda Mujiche, DealYard Hub was created with a vision of becoming a trusted bridge between India and Zimbabwe by delivering dependable services backed by transparency, efficiency, and exceptional customer support. Today, DealYard Hub proudly operates across Noida (Uttar Pradesh), Palwal (Haryana), and Peddapuram (Andhra Pradesh), providing reliable money transfer, parcel delivery, flight booking, and mobile phone solutions. As our network continues to expand, we remain committed to connecting communities through secure, affordable, and professional services while strengthening our presence across India.
